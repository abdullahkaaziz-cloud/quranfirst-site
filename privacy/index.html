Privacy policy

Qur’an First collects no data. Your reading, your voice and the apps you block stay on your iPhone.

Last updated 15 October 2026. Applies to the Qur’an First iPhone app, on TestFlight and the App Store.

What the app does not do

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No servers, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code that sends anything. The app has no network code at all.
  • It never records or keeps your voice.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your reading: your place in the muṣḥaf, the pages and days you have read, your streak and its estimate of hasanat.
  • Your settings and reminders. Reminders are notifications your iPhone schedules for itself.
  • The apps and websites you choose to block. You choose them with Apple’s Screen Time. Apple gives the app only tokens for the apps, not their names. Websites you type in are kept as you typed them.
  • Diagnostics: the words the app heard as text, how long each step took, your iPhone’s model and iOS version, and crash reports iOS hands the app.

Your iPhone keeps all of this encrypted while it is off, and after a restart until you first unlock it. Your iPhone’s backups may hold it, as they hold any app’s data. Deleting the app deletes it.

The microphone

The app listens only while you read. Your recitation is recognised on the iPhone itself, by a speech model built into the app. The audio is never saved and never sent anywhere.

Screen Time

The app uses Apple’s Screen Time (Family Controls) only to block the apps and websites you choose for yourself, until you have read. Screen Time information never leaves your iPhone. You can remove the app’s access at any time: iPhone Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access → Qur’an First.

When something leaves your iPhone

Only when you send it yourself. While the app is being tested, Settings → Testing → Export live test results makes a file of the diagnostics above and your reading progress, and opens the iPhone’s share sheet. It goes only where you choose to send it, for example by e-mail to the developer. It never holds audio, and it holds the apps you block only as counts. Nothing is sent without that step.

If you test the app with TestFlight, Apple’s TestFlight app may share crash reports and the feedback you send with the developer, under Apple’s privacy policy.

Children

The app is not designed to collect anything from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new date at the top. An app that starts collecting anything would say so here first.
